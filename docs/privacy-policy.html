1. Summary

Dao Fortune collects no personal data. The app itself has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting or tracking libraries. Your reading is calculated entirely on your device from your device's local clock and time zone.

The app lets you choose a year sign — one of the twelve branches of the Chinese zodiac — so that the plate can be read against your own position. That choice is a single branch stored in a private preferences file on your device. It is never transmitted, never included in an Android backup, and never seen by us or by Google. The app never asks for your date of birth, your name, your age or any other identifier. A year sign is not a personal identifier in the sense Play's Data safety form uses: it is one choice out of twelve, it identifies nobody, and it is deleted the moment you clear it.

The only data handled by a third party is the purchase itself: if you unlock the app, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing, and Google Play handles the payment details, your Google account and the receipt. Dao Fortune never sees or stores your card details.

The app bundles Google's Play Billing library, which brings a small number of Google Play services components with it. Those components are Google's code, operate under Google's own privacy policy, and can transmit technical diagnostics to Google in the ordinary course of talking to the Play Store. The app does not use them for analytics and does not read anything they collect.

These two flags are included in Android's device backup so that a phone transfer does not reset your purchase. They are not transmitted anywhere. The year sign is not — see below.

Your year sign

If you set a year sign, the app stores one integer — the ordinal of your branch out of twelve — in a preferences file named dao_fortune_profile.xml. Nothing else about you is recorded:

  • No birth date is collected. The app never asks for your birthday, and it cannot derive one.
  • It is kept in a different file from the purchase flags. The purchase flags live in

dao_fortune_access.xml; the year sign lives in dao_fortune_profile.xml.

  • It is excluded from backup. Android's backup rules (backup_rules.xml and

data_extraction_rules.xml) allow-list only dao_fortune_access.xml. Your year sign is left out on purpose, so it never leaves the device — not into cloud backup, not into a device-to-device transfer.

  • It is never transmitted. There is no server behind this app. The year sign is used to compute a

reading locally and is then forgotten until you change it.

  • You can erase it in one tap — the year-sign sheet in the app has a "clear" action — or by clearing

the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling.

Dates you look at

You can move the plate to any day — today, tomorrow, or a date in the next sixty days. The date you are looking at is not stored at all: it lives in memory for as long as the app is open and disappears when you close it. The forward scan of the coming sixty days is computed on your device each time the app starts; no result of it is saved or sent anywhere.

3. Permissions

Dao Fortune itself declares no permissions at all. The Google Play Billing library that the app bundles adds two at the manifest level:

Permission Where it comes from Why
INTERNET Play Billing library To reach the Play Store when you unlock the app or restore a purchase
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Play Billing library To check whether the device is online before contacting the Play Store

Neither is a runtime permission — you are never asked to approve anything. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or any sensor. The Play services components bundled with the billing library include code that could read location, but the app never asks for the location permission, so that code cannot collect anything.

4. Network activity

  • Google Play Billing: used only when you choose to unlock the app or restore a previous purchase.

Google's Play services handle the network traffic for this.

  • Google Play services diagnostics: the components bundled with the billing library may send

technical diagnostics to Google under Google's own privacy policy. The app neither reads that data nor uses it for anything.

  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no remote logging. There is no server behind this app, and the

app itself makes no network requests at all.

5. Children

Dao Fortune is not directed at children under 13. It contains no user-generated content, no chat, and no data collection. The optional year sign is a preference the reader sets on their own device; it is not transmitted and is excluded from backup.

6. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. Everything the app holds is on your device, and you can erase it yourself at any time:

  • Your year sign — clear it inside the app (the year-sign sheet has a clear action), or by clearing the

app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling.

  • The free-reading flag and the unlock — clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall.

Note that uninstalling also removes your purchase entitlement from this device; you can restore it at any time with the Restore purchase button in the app, or through your Google Play purchase history.

  • A date you were looking at — nothing to delete; it is never written to storage.

We hold no copy of anything, because we receive nothing.
