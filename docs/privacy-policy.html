1. Summary

Dao Fortune collects no personal data — in Play's sense of the word, nothing leaves your device at all. The app itself has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting or tracking libraries. Your reading is calculated entirely on your device from your device's local clock and time zone.

The app lets you choose a year sign — one of the twelve branches of the Chinese zodiac — so that the plate can be read against your own position. That choice is a single branch stored in a private preferences file on your device. It is never transmitted, never included in an Android backup, and never seen by us or by Google. A year sign is not a personal identifier in the sense Play's Data safety form uses: it is one choice out of twelve, it identifies nobody, and it is deleted the moment you clear it.

Optionally, you can also give your birth day for a more personal reading: the app then anchors every reading on the pillar of the day you were born. The birth day is optional, is stored only in the same private preferences file on your device, is excluded from Android's backup, and is never transmitted — there is no server behind this app. It is used only to compute a pillar locally. You can remove it in one tap from the same sheet where you set it.

The app can also read a dream you write yourself (梦占). The words of your dream are read on your device and are never written to storage, never transmitted and never seen by us. The picture that accompanies the reading is drawn on the device by the app itself — it is not uploaded, and no cloud service or AI model is involved. Closing the dream sheet erases the text; there is nothing left behind to delete.

The app can post notifications — a daily reading each morning, and a reminder on a day you marked. They are computed on your device at the moment they fire and are never sent from a server. The home-screen widget shows the same reading, also computed locally. If you share a reading, the app draws a picture on your device and hands it to the app you chose; the picture never passes through us.

The only data handled by a third party is the purchase itself: if you unlock the app, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing, and Google Play handles the payment details, your Google account and the receipt. Dao Fortune never sees or stores your card details.

The app bundles Google's Play Billing library, which brings a small number of Google Play services components with it. Those components are Google's code, operate under Google's own privacy policy, and can transmit technical diagnostics to Google in the ordinary course of talking to the Play Store. The app does not use them for analytics and does not read anything they collect.

Notifications

All notifications are local. The daily reading and the day reminders are scheduled with WorkManager and computed on your device at the moment they fire; nothing is sent to or received from any server. On Android 13+ the app asks for the notification permission only when you turn a notification on, and you can revoke it in Android Settings at any time — the app notices and simply stops posting.

These two flags are included in Android's device backup so that a phone transfer does not reset your purchase. They are not transmitted anywhere. The year sign and the birth day are not — see below.

Your year sign and birth day

If you set a year sign, the app stores one integer — the ordinal of your branch out of twelve — in a preferences file named dao_fortune_profile.xml. If you also set a birth day, the date is stored in the same file, as a date and nothing else — no name, no time of day, no place:

  • Both are optional. The app works fully with neither, and the birth day is never required.
  • They are kept in a different file from the purchase flags. The purchase flags live in

dao_fortune_access.xml; the year sign and birth day live in dao_fortune_profile.xml.

  • They are excluded from backup. Android's backup rules (backup_rules.xml and

data_extraction_rules.xml) allow-list only dao_fortune_access.xml. Your year sign and birth day are left out on purpose, so they never leave the device — not into cloud backup, not into a device-to-device transfer.

  • They are never transmitted. There is no server behind this app. They are used to compute a

reading locally and are then forgotten until you change them.

  • You can erase either in one tap — the year-sign sheet in the app has a clear action for both — or

by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling.

Dates you look at

You can move the plate to any day — today, tomorrow, or a date in the next sixty days. The date you are looking at is not stored at all: it lives in memory for as long as the app is open and disappears when you close it. The forward scan of the coming sixty days is computed on your device each time the app starts; no result of it is saved or sent anywhere.

The dream you write

The Dream & Omens module (梦占) lets you type what you remember of a dream, and reads it against the plate of the night it belongs to.

  • It is never written to disk. The text lives in the app's memory while the dream sheet is open and

nowhere else. There is no file, no database, no log entry, and no cache copy.

  • It is never transmitted. The app makes no network request of any kind while reading a dream. There

is no server, no model, no image service behind it.

  • The picture is drawn locally. The ink-wash plate you see is generated on your device from the dream

itself, using a fixed set of classical images. Nothing is uploaded to be drawn "in the cloud".

  • Closing the sheet erases it. Leave the dream sheet — by the button or by swiping it away — and the

text, the wake-up time and the reading are all dropped from memory.

  • The only thing that survives is the reading you were shown, and even that is not stored: it is

re-created if you read the same dream again.

3. Permissions

The app declares two permissions of its own:

Permission Why
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To post the daily reading and the day reminders you ask for. It is a runtime permission: Android asks you for it the first time you turn a notification on, and you can revoke it in Android Settings at any time — the app then simply stops posting.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To put the daily reminder back after the phone restarts.

The libraries the app bundles add these at the manifest level:

Permission Where it comes from Why
INTERNET Play Billing library To reach the Play Store when you unlock the app or restore a purchase
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Play Billing library To check whether the device is online before contacting the Play Store
com.android.vending.BILLING Play Billing library To talk to the Play Store at all
WAKE_LOCK WorkManager library To hold the device awake long enough to post a scheduled notification
FOREGROUND_SERVICE WorkManager library A fallback path inside WorkManager for expedited work; the app never starts a foreground service of its own

None of these reaches the network on its own: the app makes no request of its own, and every notification is computed on the device. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or any sensor. The Play services components bundled with the billing library include code that could read location, but the app never asks for the location permission, so that code cannot collect anything.

Sharing a reading is something you do, not something the app does on its own: the image is written to the app's private cache, handed to the app you chose, and never sent anywhere by us.

4. Network activity

  • Google Play Billing: used only when you choose to unlock the app or restore a previous purchase.

Google's Play services handle the network traffic for this.

  • Google Play services diagnostics: the components bundled with the billing library may send

technical diagnostics to Google under Google's own privacy policy. The app neither reads that data nor uses it for anything.

  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no remote logging. There is no server behind this app, and the

app itself makes no network requests at all.

5. Children

Dao Fortune is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, no accounts, no user accounts visible to anyone else, and no data collection. The optional year sign and birth day are preferences the reader sets on their own device; they are not transmitted and are excluded from backup. A dream written in the Dream & Omens module is read privately on the device and is never shared with anybody, published, or transmitted — it is not user-generated content in the sense Play's policies mean, because no other person can ever see it.

6. Your rights

Because nothing is ever transmitted to us, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. Everything the app holds is on your device, and you can erase it yourself at any time:

  • Your year sign and birth day — clear them inside the app (the year-sign sheet has a clear action

for both), or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling.

  • A dream you wrote — nothing to delete: it is never written to storage. Closing the dream sheet

erases it from memory.

  • The free-reading flag and the unlock — clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall.

Note that uninstalling also removes your purchase entitlement from this device; you can restore it at any time with the Restore purchase button in the app, or through your Google Play purchase history.

  • A date you were looking at — nothing to delete; it is never written to storage.

We hold no copy of anything, because we receive nothing.
