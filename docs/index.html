Dao Fortune

DAILY LUOPAN WISDOM

An Android app that reads the day through the twenty-four mountains of the Chinese luopan — the compass of the Daoist tradition — and tells you what the plate actually says.

  • Five readings: Today's Fortune, Windfall & Wealth, Love & Bonds, Career & Ambition, Body & Balance.
  • Favourable Days: the next sixty days scored and ranked, so you can see when to move.
  • Your year sign personalises every reading — and never leaves your phone.
  • Everything is computed on-device from the classical rules. No account, no analytics, no ads.

Privacy Policy

Dao Fortune collects no personal data. The reading is computed entirely on your device.
